About BrightStart

A role-based education support system built for a South African non-profit.

Our Story

BrightStart connects students, volunteers, and administrators through structured programmes and sessions, with attendance and performance tracked every step of the way to keep every student supported and every volunteer accountable.

Our Values

We believe education support works best when it is consistent, well-organised, and accessible — for students, guardians, and the volunteers who show up for them.
